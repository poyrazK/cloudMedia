# ADR-0009: Livestream replay defaults to public

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-03-09

## Context
Livestream content should remain discoverable after stream completion while allowing creator control.

## Decision
Automatically generate replay VOD after stream end. Default replay visibility is public, with creator override options.

## Consequences
- Pros: increases content shelf life and discoverability
- Pros: minimal extra creator steps after streaming
- Cons: risk of unintended public visibility
- Mitigation: clear visibility controls and post-processing notifications
